CASTLE ROLE · 3D

Game Help

Castle Battle & Siege Attackers fight immediate defenders first, then evaluate the castle for an open entrance, an existing breach, or a usable ladder route. Enclosed castles with practical Wall Walk access can trigger automatic ladder teams; impractical ladder routes force attackers to damage a reachable wall. Wall damage progresses through cracks, heavy masonry damage, rubble, and a real temporary breach that becomes walkable. Ladders queue one climber at a time and turn the connected Wall Walk / Tower network into an elevated combat space. Nearby Defender Swordsmen react to breaches while nearby wall Archers keep firing. Reset or battle completion restores every wall and removes all ladders.
2D Plan → 3D Castle Use 2D Plan for a clean top-down layout of walls, towers, roads, terrain, houses, and the Keep. All edits use the same game state, so switching to 3D View immediately shows the constructed result. New saves open in 2D Plan; existing saves keep their normal 3D start.
Design Philosophy You design the major castle architecture; Castle Role generates secondary details automatically. Windows, arrow slits, flags, Keep stairs, wall-access stairs/ladders, battlements, and structural details are procedural.
Modular Keep Builder P — Modular Keep. Choose width, depth, floors, roof style, corner towers, battlements, position, and rotation. Each floor is built as its own architectural level instead of stretching one model. The Keep automatically generates entrance, defensive openings, upper windows, stairs, tower details, and flags.
Diagonal Walls Wall drag now snaps to the nearest 45° direction. Supported directions cover all eight compass angles. The cyan ghost preview shows the actual final path before placement. Diagonal foundations, walkways, battlements, arrow slits, and terrain adaptation are generated automatically.
Automatic Corners Wall junctions are analyzed automatically. Square, rounded, reinforced, turret, and buttressed corner solutions are selected from wall height, thickness, angle, and importance. Straight-to-diagonal transitions retain continuous ramparts without a manual corner tool.
Automatic Castle Access Stairs, ladders, and ramps are no longer primary build-menu tools. Wall networks with walkways receive deterministic access points where required. Towers and gates are preferred access anchors; longer isolated walls receive additional access automatically. Access is recalculated whenever the castle or terrain redraws after an architectural change.
Procedural Details Lower Keep floors favor arrow slits; upper floors receive more windows. Wall arrow slits avoid important junctions and supports. Flags are intentionally rare and animate subtly. Generation is deterministic, so Save/Load reconstructs the same architectural details.
Mountains & Ranges Mountain creates a broad multi-level formation; repeated clicks grow ridges and peaks instead of a square raised tile. Mountain Range (K) uses an A → B drag preview, then generates a varied ridge with secondary peaks, valleys, rocky slopes, foothills, and lower-slope trees. Mountain forests thin out automatically as elevation and slope increase.
Terrain Editing Raise / Lower / Flatten / Smooth / Dig / Hill / Cliff remain available. Keep foundations adapt to moderate elevation differences; excessively steep new Keep placements are rejected. Walls follow edited slopes and automatic access is regenerated after terrain changes.
Walls & Towers 1 / 2 / 3 — Stone / Wooden / Reinforced Wall. Drag A → B to build a snapped straight or diagonal run. Wall thickness, height, battlements, and walkway remain player-controlled. Tower bases and roof/platform styles remain modular; defensive openings and occasional flags are automatic.
Undo / Redo Ctrl + Z — Undo · Ctrl + Y — Redo. Keep placement/configuration, wall placement, wall height, terrain editing, Move, Rotate, Delete, and other major state changes use architectural snapshots. Generated windows, slits, flags, and automatic access are regenerated from the restored parent state instead of becoming separate Undo steps.
World, Roads & Harbor The coast now reads as grass → coastal vegetation → beach/rock → wet shore → shallow water → deep ocean. Beach width varies; rocky coast, small cliffs, driftwood, shells, stones, and foam are used selectively. Road, Dirt Road, and Stone Road all support A → B drag preview and automatic intersections; roads crossing rivers render as bridges. Harbor structures still validate coastline and automatically face open water.
Long-Press Remove & Population Hold directly on a removable object for about 3 seconds to remove it without selecting the Remove tool. The circular red indicator shows hold progress; moving the pointer cancels it. Population and Army are displayed separately. Army tracks the configured/living Defender force. Long-press deletion is included in Undo history.
Camera & Mobile UI 2D Plan locks the camera overhead; right drag pans and the wheel/pinch zooms. 3D View restores orbit camera controls. The Build panel can be closed at any time and automatically collapses after choosing a tool on mobile.
