Game Help

3D Camera Left drag — Rotate the island Right drag — Pan Mouse wheel — Zoom A short click builds; dragging does not.
Walls Three wall materials are available. Adjacent walls connect without gaps. Click the same wall repeatedly to add floors and height. Gates and towers connect into fortification lines.
Moats Select Moat and click buildable land. Workers automatically walk to the tile and excavate it. Connected moat tiles fill with water when they reach a river.
Settlement Roads connect edge-to-edge automatically. Four detailed house designs are available. Farms can be placed on plains.
Mountains & Mines N — Place a mountain. Click the same mountain repeatedly to grow it taller and more detailed. M — Build a mine on a natural or player-built mountain.
Nature Editing T — Add a tree. R — Carve a river tile. L — Fill a river/water tile back into land. X — Remove trees, rocks, huts, and player construction.
World Templates Use Templates in the top bar to start from a prepared layout. Blank Island, River Citadel, Mountain Hold, and Farming Village are included.
Save Changes autosave after building. Save / Load uses browser local storage. Reset returns to a natural seeded island.
