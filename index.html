01

Castle Building

Planning, walls, keeps, bridges, and procedural castle systems.

Walls, Towers & Diagonal Building
1 / 2 / 3 — Stone / Wooden / Reinforced Wall. Drag A → B to build a snapped straight or diagonal run. Wall drag snaps to the nearest 45° direction across all eight compass angles. The cyan ghost preview shows the final path before placement. Wall thickness, height, battlements, and walkway remain player-controlled. Tower bases and roof/platform styles remain modular. Wall junctions are analyzed automatically so straight-to-diagonal transitions receive continuous corners, ramparts, and terrain-aware foundations.
Modular Keep & Architecture
P — Modular Keep. Choose width, depth, floors, roof style, corner towers, battlements, position, and rotation. Each floor is built as its own architectural level. The Keep automatically generates its entrance, openings, upper windows, stairs, tower details, and flags. Stone Style changes the castle between Limestone, Dark Stone, Sandstone, and Rough Frontier without rebuilding geometry. Walls and towers stay upright on uneven terrain; stepped foundations extend downward instead of tilting the structure.
Tower Bridges & Automatic Wall Access
D — Tower Bridge: select one main tower, then a compatible second tower. Hover shows a green/red bridge preview. Choose Stone or Wooden bridge in Castle Architecture settings. Selecting an existing tower pair again removes its bridge. Wall networks with Top Walkway automatically receive stairs, ramps, or ladders where vertical access is required. Tower Bridges and generated access join the elevated battle navigation network, including movement after defenders gain wall access.
2D Plan → 3D Castle
Use 2D Plan for a clean top-down layout of walls, towers, roads, terrain, houses, and the Keep. All edits use the same game state, so switching to 3D View immediately shows the constructed result. New saves open in 2D Plan; existing saves keep their normal 3D start.
Procedural Castle Details
Lower Keep floors favor arrow slits; upper floors receive more windows. Wall arrow slits avoid important junctions and supports. Buttresses and machicolations are generated contextually from wall height, thickness, connections, tower importance, and terrain. Flags are intentionally rare and animate subtly. Generation is deterministic, so Save/Load reconstructs the same architectural details.
What You Control vs. What Is Automatic
You control major architecture: walls, towers, roof style, global stone style, Tower Bridges, wall height, thickness, battlements, and walkways. Foundations, corner solutions, wall access, buttress spacing, machicolations, masonry variation, defensive openings, and structural supports are derived automatically.
02

World & Settlement

Citizens, farms, terrain, rivers, roads, coasts, and harbor progression.

Living Settlement & Farm Upgrades
Residential cells contain denser medieval neighborhoods with small homes, alleys, wells, fences, timber framing, windows, chimneys, and yard details. Visible citizens wander between homes, roads, farms, and village destinations. Farmers travel from nearby housing to their assigned fields, work, then return home. Farm and Cow Barn are four-level upgradeable agriculture buildings. Select one in Inspect mode and use its Upgrade card in Build Settings. Farm upgrades add crop beds, irrigation, storage and field details; Cow Barn upgrades add livestock, feeding infrastructure, larger barns, stone foundations, and a Level 4 stockyard.
Terrain Editing & Mountains
Raise / Lower / Flatten / Smooth / Hill / Cliff remain available. Lower is the supported tool for decreasing terrain elevation. Mountain creates a broad multi-level formation; repeated clicks grow ridges and peaks instead of a square raised tile. Mountain Range (K) uses an A → B drag preview, then generates a varied ridge with secondary peaks, valleys, rocky slopes, foothills, and lower-slope trees. Keep foundations adapt to moderate elevation differences; excessively steep new Keep placements are rejected. Walls follow edited slopes and automatic access is regenerated.
Water & Terrain Templates
River tiles contain flowing water immediately wherever the River tool is used; they do not require connection to another water source. Natural trees, rocks, huts, and mountain props can be replaced directly by river water; constructed structures must still be removed first. Terrain Templates provide editable Rolling Plains, Twin Rivers, Alpine Basin, Coastal Cliffs, Forest Highlands, Marsh Island, and Terraced Hills starts.
World, Roads & Harbor
The coast transitions through grass, coastal vegetation, beach/rock, wet shore, shallow water, and deep ocean with selective cliffs, stones, driftwood, shells, and foam. Road, Dirt Road, and Stone Road support A → B drag preview and automatic intersections; roads crossing rivers render as bridges. The unified Harbor validates coastline, automatically faces ocean water, and upgrades through four visually distinct levels from Landing Dock to Grand Harbor.
03

Battle & Economy

Siege behavior, army progression, resources, food, and population.

Castle Battle & Siege
Army sizes can be typed directly or changed with ±. Swordsmen, Spearmen, Archers, and Crossbowmen have different health, reach, speed, damage, and firing cadence. Attackers evaluate open entrances, breaches, ladders, wall height, and nearby defenders. Spearmen join melee assaults while Crossbowmen provide slower heavy ranged fire. Wall damage progresses through damaged, heavy, partially collapsed, and fully breached states before navigation opens through the wall. Army Camps have four upgrade levels from Field Camp through Royal War Camp. Each level changes the 3D design while Siege ladders, Wall Walk combat, breach response, and battle-only castle restoration remain active.
Settlement Economy
Farms produce Grain, Orchards produce Apples, Cow Barns produce Food, Mines produce Stone, and Huts/Trees contribute Wood. Windmills consume Grain to make Flour; Markets convert part of that Flour into ready Food. Population consumes edible reserves every deterministic economy tick. Construction costs Wood/Stone. Enable Free Build in God Mode to build without resource costs. Resource totals, net rates, storage capacity, and food-shortage state appear inside Build. Economy inventory is saved with the world; older saves without economy data receive backward-compatible starter resources.
Population & Long-Press Remove
Population and Army are displayed separately. Army tracks the configured/living Defender force. Hold directly on a removable object for about 3 seconds to remove it without selecting the Remove tool. The circular red indicator shows hold progress; moving the pointer cancels it. Long-press deletion is included in Undo history.
04

Controls & Recovery

Camera behavior, mobile interaction, and ways to reverse mistakes.

Camera & Mobile UI
2D Plan locks the camera overhead; drag to pan and pinch to zoom. On desktop, right drag pans and the wheel zooms. 3D View uses one finger to orbit and two fingers to pan or zoom. The Build panel can be closed at any time and automatically collapses after choosing a tool on mobile.
Undo / Redo
Ctrl + Z — Undo · Ctrl + Y — Redo. Keep placement/configuration, wall placement, wall height, terrain editing, Move, Rotate, Delete, and other major state changes use architectural snapshots. Generated windows, slits, flags, and automatic access are regenerated from the restored parent state instead of becoming separate Undo steps.
