Game Help

Terrain Editing Raise / Lower — change elevation. Flatten — level the brush toward its center. Smooth — blend nearby heights. Dig — excavate deeper terrain. Create Hill / Cliff — rounded or sharp elevation. Brush Size and Strength are adjustable in the build panel.
Terrain-aware Castle Buildings follow edited terrain height. Walls use slope-aware connections between different elevations. This supports hill castles, mountain castles, and river-side castles.
Castle Access Stone Stairs, Wooden Stairs, Ramp, and Ladder are available. Access pieces must be placed next to a Wall, Gate, or Tower. They automatically snap toward the fortification and match its height.
Walls & Towers Drag a wall from A to B for a snapped continuous line. Walls now use heavier foundations, masonry/timber details, double-sided parapets, merlons, and real rampart walkways. Stone / Wooden / Reinforced wall materials remain independently configurable. Towers have five bases and five top modules.
Undo / Redo Ctrl + Z — Undo Ctrl + Y — Redo Build, Delete, Move, Rotate, Terrain Edit, and Wall/Tower Height changes are recorded.
Selection Click a structure to make its grid tile the active selection. Use the arrow controls to move it one tile. Rotate turns supported structures 90°. [ / ] lower or raise selected Wall/Tower floors.
Rivers & Water River tiles now connect into a continuous channel with lowered riverbed and natural banks. The river surface is animated so the water visibly flows through the world. Workers dig queued moat tiles and connected moats fill when they reach the river. River and Land tools still reshape waterways.
Residential Clusters Housing tools no longer place one oversized building. Cottage, House, Manor, and Villa now create small groups of homes with paths, chimneys, windows, fences, wells, and courtyards.
Camera Wall and terrain tools reserve left-drag for construction/editing. With other tools: Left drag — Rotate camera. Right drag — Pan · Mouse wheel — Zoom.
